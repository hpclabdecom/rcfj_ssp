# Experiments, hardware and results

What was run, on which machines, under which protocol, and the numbers obtained. All numbers come from the files in
`results/`; every table below is either copied from `results/TABLES.md` (generated by
`python experimentos/analise_campanhas.py`) or from `python rcfjssp_campaign.py --report`. K = Kasapidis et al.
(2025); "published" always means the values printed in their Tables 7–10.

## 1. Benchmark and comparison units

* **Instances.** The 90 instance files of K (`instances/README.md`). Experiments 1–3 (80 files, 110 table cells)
  are used only to validate the transcription of the model; Experiment 4 (`lcbrtu_0`–`lcbrtu_9`, 98–156
  operations, all resource classes) is the evaluation set of every heuristic campaign.
* **Published reference values for Experiment 4** (K, Table 10): a lower bound and a makespan from CP Optimizer
  20.1 at 1800 s (one run), and the best of ten ALNS-CP runs with one *Time (s)* figure per instance. That figure is
  copied numerically as the wall-clock budget T_i of our corresponding runs: 623.45, 179.86, 476.44, 495.88, 604.69,
  643.44, 231.97, 283.92, 800.39 and 206.70 s for `lcbrtu_0`–`lcbrtu_9` (`T_ALNS` in `experimentos/rcfjssp_lns.py`;
  published values in `rcfjssp_reference.py`).
* **Units of comparison.** Against the published ALNS-CP column (an external attained-value reference obtained on
  different hardware with a different solver), the best of ten runs and the median of ten runs. Between our own
  methods, the matched comparison on the same platform, the same total thread allocation, the same budgets and
  ten seeds: PT-W 8×1 against TW-LNS 4×2 (eight threads) on both machines, PT-W 4×2 against TW-LNS 4×2 on the i7,
  and PT-W 16×1 against TW-LNS 8×2 (sixteen threads) on the Ryzen. Results of the two computers are never pooled.
* **Verification.** Every reported schedule was re-checked by the solver-free checker (`rcfjssp_audit.py`, checks
  A–E), which reads only the instance and the stored schedule. Passing establishes feasibility and the reported
  makespan under the implemented semantics, not optimality.

## 5. Calibration and replica-layout screen

**Calibration grid** (`final/calibracao`). The configuration is ranked by the mean relative percentage deviation
100(C − b_i)/b_i over its 100 runs, where b_i is the best makespan found for instance i over the whole grid. The
selected pair is θ = 8 s and ρ₀ = 0.25, mean RPD 8.803 % (`escolha.json`). The runner used in the grid contained the
time-window, machine, material and critical-operation neighbourhoods; the two selected values were transferred to the
time-window-only TW-LNS without retuning.

## 8. Statistical procedures (as implemented in `experimentos/analise_campanhas.py`)

* Per instance: exact two-sided Mann–Whitney U test with average ranks for ties and full enumeration of the
  C(20, 10) = 184 756 assignments; omitted when all twenty observations are equal; Holm correction over the
  non-degenerate instances of the same comparison.
* Across instances: stratified permutation test (method labels permuted within each instance, 20 000 resamples,
  fixed seed) on the average of the per-instance differences of means, one-sided in favour of PT-W.
* The win/tie/loss count of a comparison is defined from the two per-instance medians. Global p-values are reported
  separately for each comparison and are not adjusted across comparisons.
